03 / BEFORE YOU TAKE THE CONTROLS

A LITTLE CLARITY.
A CONFIDENT START.

View the original course flyer ↗
How long is the training?+

The advertised training duration is two weeks. Contact Haven to confirm the next intake and your chosen course’s schedule.

What should I bring?+

Bring your own blankets and toiletries. Food and accommodation are free. Ask the team about any enrolment documents and additional requirements before travelling.

What about certificates?+

Haven’s flyer advertises internationally recognized certificates. Ask the team about the awarding body, accreditation and recognition for the country or employer you have in mind.

Will Haven help me find work?+

Haven offers job-hunting assistance. Contact the institute to discuss the available support. Employment is not guaranteed.

How do I secure a place?+

Choose a course and send the team an enquiry on WhatsApp, or call either number below. They can confirm fees, availability, payment arrangements and your next steps.
