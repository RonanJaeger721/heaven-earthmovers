# Haven — S-tier direction

Emotion: possibility, scale and confidence. Audience: people considering machinery operator training. Actual conversion: a visitor-reviewed WhatsApp enquiry, never a claimed booking.

Three opening concepts considered:
1. Industrial showroom: isolated equipment and price controls. Clear, but this was the previous design and lacked atmosphere.
2. Operator cockpit: full-screen instrument controls. Rejected because verified machine specifications and usable cockpit imagery are absent; decorative dashboard would mislead.
3. Dawn at the earthmoving site: cinematic illustrative scene, compact human-centred copy, then an engineering outline that resolves into the machine. Chosen for scale, strong contrast and a useful handoff into courses.

Primary methods: ST-06 cinematic scene momentum and ST-07 shared-anchor progressive story. ST-04 course selection supports conversion without becoming the whole identity. The triangle in Haven’s logo informs angled boundaries. Typography stays restrained after the user's explicit correction: approximately 34–50px hero, 30–44px section headings.

Motion: one brief scene entrance, selective copy reveal, scroll-linked environmental depth and a deterministic pinned outline-to-machine reveal. The precision stage exposes tappable mechanical-part labels. Forward/backward scrolling recomputes stage from the same progress. Mobile uses explicit three-stage controls without extended pinning. Reduced motion has a complete still composition and tap controls.

Imagery: generated dawn scene and generated machine cutout, clearly labelled illustrative. No invented client facility, trainee, credentials, fleet or testimonials. Flyer facts and contacts remain unchanged. This is a designed image transition with an illustrative SVG outline, not a real 3D model or mechanical simulation.
